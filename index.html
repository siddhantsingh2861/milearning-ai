Try it

  1. Click then tap the notification on the phone to see the resources HR assigned.
  2. Tap a time chip like Under 10 min to filter today's picks by the time you have.
  3. Tap the mic next to the ask bar. It fills a Hinglish question. Hit send and pick a level.
  4. Start a new chat and tap Continue: Team Management. It skips the level question because HR already set it.
  5. On the home tile, open a pick marked HR assigned, tap Continue, then choose Finished it. The goal bar moves from 1/4 to 2/4.

The problem

L&D spends heavily on creating content for the central LMS, in-house or through external vendors. Most of it is generic, so it rarely fits what a given employee actually needs. Recommendations reach people as links they don't open, nobody knows what was finished, and individual learning plans stay fragmented. Keeping them on track needs repeated HRBP or L&D follow-up.

The solution

Instead of building more content, MiLearning.AI brings existing, relevant content to each employee, free wherever possible. It works two ways: employees ask for what they need, and HR pushes what each person needs.

Design decisions

  • Voice first. Many users would rather send a voice note than type in English. The composer takes Hindi, English or Hinglish.
  • Time chips. People learn on the commute or between meetings. They choose by the time they have more than by topic.
  • Cost and certificate up front. "Is it free?" and "Do I get a certificate?" decide whether someone taps. Home cards show Free / Paid / Free to audit, Certificate, and a हिंदी badge for Hindi content.
  • Visible progress against the HR goal. Formal, appraisal-linked targets drive action. The tile shows "1/4 done · due 31 Mar".
  • Gentler level question. "Just starting / I know the basics / I'm quite confident / Not sure, you decide" replaces Beginner/Expert. People tend to undersell themselves on those labels. If HR already set the level, the question is skipped.
  • Completion check-in. External platforms don't report completion back. A one-tap "Finished / Halfway / Not started" on return gives HR completion data, not just clicks.
  • One hub, grouped by need. Saved, History and Assigned sit under a single "My Learning" button. Saved and History are grouped by learning need, matching how people think: "the Excel stuff", "the team management stuff".
  • Bigger tap targets and text for budget Android phones with large font settings.

What's mocked

  • All courses, ratings, providers and dates are placeholders.
  • Voice input fills a fixed Hinglish sample. A real build needs speech-to-text that supports Hindi (hi-IN).
  • "Continue to [platform]" jumps straight to the check-in. A real build opens the link and shows the check-in when the user returns.
  • Share, Applications and other app tiles only show a toast.

How it connects to the agent

The underlying agent runs in Microsoft Teams. It captures the learning need in chat, and a Power Automate flow posts the recommendations back as an Adaptive Card. This mockup explores the same experience as a native module inside the employee app.
